Yieldvale Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · App package: com.yieldvale.app

In short: Yieldvale has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your fields, expenses and notes stay on your phone. When you use a feature that needs our server (the sowing decision, weather, map layers or disease scan), the app sends only what that feature needs (an approximate location, or one leaf photo), uses it to answer, and does not keep it linked to you.

What stays only on your phone

Field names, crops, sowing dates, field boundaries (walked or drawn on the map), field plans and water outlets you mark, expenses and observation notes are stored in a database on your device. They are never uploaded. We have no copy. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What is sent to our server, and why

DataWhenWhyKept?
Approximate location (rounded on your phone to a grid of about 2 km) When you open Sowing Decision, Weather, Disease Scan or Mandi prices, turn on the map's wind or cyclone layers, or open the Field Planner (to look up the usual wind) To give the weather, sowing advice and cyclone distance for your area Not stored in our database. Our hosting provider's request logs, which include the rounded location and your IP address, are kept for troubleshooting for no more than 30 days, then deleted.
A leaf photo Only when you scan a leaf and this build has no on-phone disease model To identify the likely disease The photo is analysed in memory and discarded. If the result is confident, we add one anonymous count to your district's outbreak tally (district, disease name, confidence, time). Nothing else, and nothing that identifies you or your phone.
A price you report (crop, price per quintal, where you sold, date) Only when you tap "I sold at..." in Mandi prices To show farmers in your district what others were paid, as a median Kept with your district only (worked out from your rounded location). No name, phone number or device identifier is stored, so a report cannot be traced back to you.
Crop and acreage you type When you use the insurance premium calculator To calculate the premium Not stored.

All communication with our server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Other services involved

  • Map pictures are downloaded by your phone directly from OpenStreetMap, Esri (satellite view) and NASA (cloud and rain layers). These services see your IP address and which part of the map you are viewing, under their own privacy policies.
  • Our server gets mandi prices from the government's AGMARKNET data on data.gov.in, weather from Open-Meteo, cyclone data from GDACS (United Nations / European Commission) and satellite image times from NASA, sending them only the rounded location, never anything that identifies you.
  • Hosting: our server runs on Railway, which processes requests on our behalf.

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. There is no tracking across other apps or websites.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (only while you use the app): for the features above, and to measure a field when you walk its boundary. Boundaries stay on your phone.
  • Camera: only when you tap "Take photo" in Disease Scan.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on geofence alerts, to tell you when you enter or leave a field.

You can refuse any permission; only the feature that needs it stops working.

Children

Yieldvale is made for adult farmers and is not directed at children.

Your choices and deletion

Everything personal lives on your phone, so uninstalling the app deletes it. We keep nothing linked to you on our server. If you have a question or want us to confirm anything about your data, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with the change described plainly.
